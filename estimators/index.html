    Estimators¶

    Every estimator is a pure function of daily sufficient statistics, split into features(stats, days) (a days × K matrix of additive daily features) and fit(sums) (ADR 0004). Roles (principal, control, robustness, diagnostic, negative control) are assigned in ofs.estimators.registry from config/estimation.yaml.

    H0 — signed flow¶

    X is the cumulative deseasonalised signed notional on windows of Δ, centred day by day in proportion to volume (ADR 0015, deviation D4). Each window loses m_d V, its volume times the day's imbalance m_d = net flow / volume. A day-level imbalance riding on bursts of activity is then not read as memory; the uncentred flow reads a memoryless 5 % daily imbalance as H0 ≈ 0.98.

    • Principal — mixed fBm (mixed_fbm_vc): E[(X_{t+τ} − X_t)²] = σ1² τ^{2H0} + σ2² τ.
    • Fitted by weighted NLS on log-variances, with weights ∝ 1/τ (inverse variance under the Brownian null), for τ from Δ to 4 h.
    • Both terms take their exact expected shape after the daily centring, a bridge: g_H(m) = m^{2H} − 2(m/N)A(m) + (m/N)² N^{2H}.
    • The fractional component must explain, at 4 h, more than twice the sampling error of the variance there (2·sqrt(4m/3n), floor 5 %). Otherwise the flow is Brownian and H0 = 1/2 (ADR 0008).
    • Control — quadratic-variation ratio on (Δ, 2Δ, 4Δ), the paper's own estimator H = ½ log2[(V(4Δ) − 2V(2Δ)) / (V(2Δ) − 2V(Δ))], on the same flow. The simulation study chose it over the sign autocorrelation, which was biased by 0.033.
    • Robustness:
    • the other windows Δ;
    • no deseasonalisation;
    • τ up to 1 h;
    • uncentred, the pre-registered principal on the uncentred flow.
    • Diagnostics:
    • the simple fBm slope, which reproduces the paper's bias rising with Δ;
    • DFA-2;
    • the event-time sign autocorrelation, C(ℓ) ~ ℓ^{−γ}, H0 = 1 − γ/2, fitted on the leading run of lag bins significantly above zero.
    • Negative control: signs permuted within each day, then the same centring and fit; it must give 1/2 (deviation D3). The shuffled sign autocorrelation is no control: its flat autocorrelation gives H0 = 1 by construction.

    H_vol — volume rate¶

    v_k = V_k / Δ, deseasonalised. Principal: moments m(q, ℓ) = mean|v_{k+ℓ} − v_k|^q ~ ℓ^{q H} with a linearity check of ζ(q). Control: the same on log v. Also computed: the paper's autocovariance-GMM of increments truncated at 3 sd, which models white observation noise explicitly.

    H_σ — volatility¶

    The theory predicts the extraday roughness (paper, Remark 4.2): daily horizon primary.

    • GJR moments of log σ increments on daily 5-minute RV (lags 1–50 days); TSRV variant.
    • Whittle (Fukasawa, Takabatake & Westphal): increments of log RV modelled as increments of a day-averaged fBm plus white measurement noise, spectral density f(λ) = ν²[4 c_H (1 − cos λ)² Σ_k |λ + 2πk|^{−2H−3} + r (1 − cos λ)/π]; its bootstrap re-estimates on block-resampled series without cross-block increments.
    • p-variation (Cont & Das): H = 1/p* where the p-variation stops depending on the step.
    • Intraday horizon (15-minute local variances of 10-second returns): descriptive.
    • Long history (spec §4): the same daily estimators on up to 5 years of daily RV rebuilt from monthly 1-minute klines (ofs history, sample history, variant daily_5y); a robustness variant of H2 (ADR 0011).

    δ — impact¶

    Proxy A: runs of same-sign trades less than g seconds apart, I = ε (log p_last − log p_first), fit E[I | Q] = A Q^δ on log-binned notional (control: on run duration). Proxy B: aggregate impact against the normalised imbalance (descriptive).

    What the simulations say¶

    Quick profile (ofs simulate study --profile quick, 2026-11-09: 40 replications per cell, 99 bootstrap draws, 20 simulated days, 120 for H_σ):

    Exponent Finding Consequence
    H0 mixed fBm (1/τ weights): |bias| ≤ 0.02 in all 20 cells (H0 0.6-0.9, noise/signal 0-2) principal kept
    H0 equal weights in log space: same bias, twice the sd; spurious H on Brownian data weights 1/τ (ADR 0008)
    H0 sign ACF on Lillo-Mike-Farmer signs: biased low for H0 ≥ 0.75 (finite-size effect of the model) control choice ruled in the pre-registration
    H_vol moments and log-moments biased by observation noise; autocovariance-GMM unbiased up to noise/signal 1 rule in the pre-registration §4.1
    H_σ GJR on daily RV biased up by ≈ 0.1 everywhere; Whittle much closer on 365 days rule in the pre-registration §4.1
    δ proxy A unbiased —
    all 95 % bootstrap CIs too narrow on short samples: H0 coverage 72 % (20 days), 78 % (60), 85 % (120) pre-registered calibration sd_inflation from the full study

    The coverage problem is the known weakness of block bootstraps under long memory: the bootstrap spread approaches the true one as the sample grows (true sd / bootstrap sd 1.48 at 20 days, 1.13 at 120), and longer blocks do not help (1.38 with 30-day blocks at 120 days: fewer blocks make the bootstrap itself noisier). The full profile therefore simulates 365-day samples, like the real ones, and reports sd_ratio per cell.

    Full profile (2026-11-10): the pre-registered decisions¶

    82 cells, 200 replications of 365 days, 199 bootstrap draws (ofs simulate decide --profile full; worst |bias| over the cells of the plausible region, noise/signal ≤ 1):

    Exponent Candidates (worst |bias|) Decision
    H0 mixed fBm 0.001 principal
    H0 control sign ACF 0.033, quadratic-variation ratio 0.000 QV ratio replaces the sign ACF
    H_vol autocovariance GMM 0.014, moments 0.079 GMM principal, moments control
    H_σ GJR 0.121, Whittle 0.030 (H_σ ∈ [0.05, 0.15]) neither within 0.02: the rule kept GJR; deviation D1 makes Whittle principal; H2 reported as limited
    δ proxy A 0.000 principal
    calibration median true sd / bootstrap sd over 39 cells k = 1.14 at registration (H0 coverage 72-94 % raw, 80-96 % with k); k = 1.06 after the volume-centred H0 of D4 (H0 bias 0.0005, coverage 89-96 % raw, 91.5-98 % with k)

    Controls that fail the bias rule (moments for H_vol, GJR for H_σ) are reported but do not decide whether a result counts (deviation D2, config/deviations.md).

    The H_σ result is itself a finding: on one year of daily data, roughness near H = 0.1 cannot be estimated within 0.02 by either estimator; the five-year history variant (daily_5y) of the specification curve shows how much a longer sample helps.

    The definitive tables come from make sim (profile full) and are part of the report.
