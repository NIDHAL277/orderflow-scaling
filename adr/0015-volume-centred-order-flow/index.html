    ADR 0015 — H0 on the order flow centred day by day in proportion to volume¶

    Status: accepted (2026-11-12). Amends ADR 0008 point (1) and its negative control; recorded as deviations D3 and D4 (config/deviations.md).

    Context: on the development year (2024-11-01 to 2025-10-31, 60 spot and 59 USD-M pairs) the shuffled-sign negative control failed. The pre-registered expectation is H0 = 1/2. The principal estimator gave 0.80 on spot and 0.79 on USD-M (0 of 60 spot pairs within 0.05 of 0.5). The sign-autocorrelation version gave 1.00 everywhere.

    • The sign-autocorrelation control cannot work: shuffled signs have a flat autocorrelation, a power law fitted to it has exponent 0, and H0 = 1 − γ/2 = 1 by construction.
    • The flow control failed because of how it was centred. The shuffle permutes signs within each day, so each day keeps its net imbalance m_d = net flow / volume, about 5 % of volume on BTC. The shuffled flow therefore drifts in proportion to traded volume: m_d · V per window. The control removed the day's mean increment, a drift uniform in time, which leaves m_d (V − mean V). That residue inherits the clustering of trading activity and reads as memory.
    • The same drift is in the real flow, which the principal did not centre at all (ADR 0008: "the daily net imbalance is part of the memory"). In simulation, signs with no memory but a random daily imbalance of sd 5 % give H0 ≈ 0.97–0.99 with that estimator.
    • The simulation study and the property test missed both problems because their trades arrive uniformly in time with independent sizes. With no activity clustering, the two centrings agree.

    Decision:

    1. The volume-centred flow. The reducer removes each day's imbalance from every window in proportion to the window's volume: F − (ΣF / ΣV) V. It does so for the signed flow (deseasonalised and raw) and for the shuffled flow. It stores the variance sums in their own table, flow_var_vc, with a version of its own (flow_vc), so adding it did not recompute the other pass-2 statistics.
    2. The principal (mixed_fbm_vc) fits the mixed fBm to that flow, and so do its window, no-seasonality and τmax variants, the control (quadratic-variation ratio) and the diagnostics. Removing the day's net increment shrinks the variance at large τ like a bridge. With constant activity the expected mean squared increment of lag m, out of N windows, is exactly g_H(m) = m^{2H} − 2 (m/N) A(m) + (m/N)² N^{2H}, where A(m) = [Σ_{u=N−m+1..N} u^{2H} − Σ_{u=1..m−1} u^{2H}] / (N − m + 1). For H = 1/2 this is the Brownian bridge m (1 − m/N). The fit uses g_H and g_{1/2} instead of the pure powers. They are tabulated on a grid of H and interpolated by a spline; a fit takes about 10 ms.
    3. The negative control is the same fit on the shuffled, volume-centred flow. The shuffled sign autocorrelation becomes a diagnostic.
    4. The pre-registered principal stays in the specification curve as the H0 variant uncentred.

    Checks: g_H equals the exact covariance of a centred fBm to machine precision (unit test). On simulated fGn centred day by day, with 60 days, the bridge-aware fit recovers H:

    True H Centred, plain fit Centred, bridge-aware fit
    0.7 0.680 0.702
    0.8 0.770 0.803
    0.9 0.867 0.901

    A property test with clustered activity and a daily imbalance but no memory requires H0 = 1/2 for both the signed and the shuffled volume-centred flow. The H0 cells of the simulation study are re-run with the new principal (scenario version 2), and the pre-registered acceptance rule decides.

    Consequences:

    • H0 measures within-day persistence of the flow net of each day's imbalance. A day-level imbalance, which the uncentred estimator counted as memory, no longer counts.
    • On the 8 pair-markets above, H0 drops by 0.01–0.085 (median 0.03).
    • With genuine memory the centring removes part of the low-frequency signal, and the bridge-aware fit corrects that exactly under constant activity.
    • Under clustered activity the correction is approximate. The remaining effect, about 0.02 in the Lillo–Mike–Farmer simulation, is within the simulation's tolerance and is measured by the re-run study.
    • Every development-sample estimate, test and forecast that uses H0 is recomputed before the config-frozen tag.

    Outcome on the development year (re-run of 2026-11-14, all 119 pair-markets):

    • The negative control has a median of 0.500 on spot and 0.499 on USD-M, with 90 % and 97 % of the pairs within 0.05 of 1/2. The rest are fits without a fractional part, whose intervals contain 1/2.
    • H0 hardly moves on spot (median 0.704 against 0.699) and falls on USD-M (0.584 against 0.619). The estimate on 8 pair-markets above had suggested a fall of 0.03 on both markets.
    • On 27 of the 59 USD-M pairs, and on no spot pair, the fit puts no weight on the Brownian part: the variance of the centred flow is a single power law from 10 seconds to four hours. The quadratic-variation ratio falls with the window on USD-M (median 0.64 at 10 seconds, 0.60 at 60 seconds), so the exponent depends on the time scale there. The mixed model of the theory fits the spot flow better than the perpetual one (median log error 0.05 against 0.12).
    • Three decisions of the development tests changed, in both directions: H1 on USD-M from undecided to compatible, H3 on both markets from undecided to rejected (config/deviations.md, D4).
